  ⚠️  "Could not read mGameInfo"? → emulator not rooted 🔓,
      Township never opened 🏘️, or wrong device selected 📲.

  ② Pick a tab & tick  🎛️✅    → choose what to unlock/edit

     reset when the server updates — normal, takes 10 seconds).
