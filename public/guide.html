🚀 Getting Started

Do these five steps in order. Nothing is written to your device until you press 💾 Save & push.

Prerequisites

🖥️ Prepare your machine

  • Windows 10 / 11 PC — the tool is a windowed desktop app.
  • An Android emulator with ADB (MEmu, LDPlayer, BlueStacks…). Rooted gives the full feature set.
  • Township installed and signed in on that emulator.
  • Free disk space — at least 1 GB free in %TEMP%.
Launch

🚀 Start the tool

Extract IGG.VIP.TOOL.zip (right-click → Extract All), then run IGG VIP TOOL\IGG VIP TOOL.exe — no installer and no setup. One window opens and the workspace is ready.

  • Keep the window open for the whole session; it is the only place edits are applied.
  • Nothing is written to your device until you press 💾 Save & push.
  • If it closes immediately with “return code -1”, that is free disk space — see 07 Troubleshooting.
Connect

🔌 Connect & load

Start your emulator, open Township, then press 🔌 Connect & load in the top bar. The tool finds your save automatically — it searches every known layout (saves/, files/, package root) instead of assuming one path.

  • Your city appears in the Workspace panel.
  • The System Console fills with what just happened.
  • All tabs unlock with your real data.
Backup first, always. Press ⬇️ Export current XML before your first edit. It returns your save exactly as it arrived — that is your undo button for life.
The Golden Rule

💾 Everything queues — one button applies it

Every tab works the same way. You tick, type or drag — the tool queues the change. Nothing touches your save until you press the single green button:

Select / edit  →  queued  →  💾 Save & push  →  applied + written to device

Queue as many features as you like across different tabs — they all land in one push, and the push runs every safety gate first.

After the push

🎮 Open the game

Force-stop Township, reopen it and let it finish loading. If the save was rejected by the game, the tool would have refused to push it in the first place — that is exactly what the gates are for.

🖥️ The Workspace

Three permanent zones, everywhere you go.

🧭 Top Bar

Language selector (20 languages), the 🔌 Connect & load button and the green 💾 Save & push button. Save & push always shows a badge like Save & push 3 — that is how many queued actions are about to land.

🗂️ Tab Bar

Eight primary tabs are always visible. The rest live behind a ⋯ More overflow so labels never wrap or squeeze. Everything is searchable inside each tab.

📜 System Console

Every action writes a line here — what was read, what was written, what was refused and why. If something looks wrong, read the console first.

Language switch — pick your language from the top bar. All 14 tabs translate live; the System Console messages are English and Vietnamese by design.

⛵ Regatta Clone-based

Complete a week's worth of regatta tasks by cloning tasks your own save already finished — never fabricating ids, scores or dates.

🎯 How to use

The batch is capped so one push never writes more than a strong real week holds, and timestamps always land inside the live window, in the past, strictly ordered.

⚙️ Upgrades Factories · Trains · Islands

Raise factory, train and island levels to a target — per row or all at once.

🎯 How to use

“No workshop changed”? Every requested row already sits at the target — that is a no-op, reported honestly instead of pretending success.

🔓 Unban Center — The Copy Workflow

Copy a friend's city into your own account. This is the most powerful tab, so it is also the most careful one — it runs in staged order, exactly the way a reference tool does.

🧭 The 3 steps

  • 1 · Friends list — pick someone from your friends, or paste a cityId manually.
  • 2 · Download city — the server fetches that city from Playrix and keeps the session.
  • 3 · Restore — the server merges and hands back a file ready to push.
Multi-friend is pick → fetch → copy, repeat. Each new FetchCity simply replaces the donor — your own cityId, session and device are never touched.

🎚️ The three restore buttons

  • 🧮 Basic stats
    Level, money and city numbers only. No town, no lifetime history.
  • 🏙️ Full city
    The town layout plus its history — but never your name, stickers, avatars or co-op.
  • 🌐 All
    Everything the reference tool copies, in one restore.
The town follows the friend — your identity does not. Name, stickers, avatars, badges and co-op membership stay yours in every mode. That split is deliberate.

staged copy — the recommended path

Copying a whole city in one go is what the reference tools stopped doing. Ours splits it into three separate pushes with the game opened in between — each step unlocks only after the previous one actually pushed.

Step 1

🧮 Basic stats

Applied. Now open the game, play the whole tutorial, reach at least level 3 and unlock the Zoo, let it finish loading — then come back.

Step 2

🏘️ Town + Zoo

Applied. Open the game once more and let it load fully, then come back for the final step.

Step 3

🧩 Advanced blocks

The last pieces. When the tab says All 3 steps are copied, you are done.

⚠️ New accounts die on day one. Every save ever banned on file was under one hour old. If your account is new, play it first — finish the tutorial, reach day two — before you copy a big city onto it. Read section 06.

🧭 Co-op readiness checklist

Before you join a co-op and type anything, the Unban tab shows a checklist read from your own save. It never blocks a push — it simply tells you the truth:

🏷️ Your town name

Name the town in game first.

💬 Stickers owned

Sending one you don't own bans. Unlock it first.

🎓 Tutorial finished

Clear the open tutorial flags.

📅 Past day one

Give the account age before it gets seen.

Device standing cannot be read from any file. A once-banned device bans again no matter how clean the save looks — use one clean device or ID per account.

🎮 New Account — Build a Level-1 City

A wipe alone never creates a new city: with no save, the game asks Playrix which city belongs to this device — and gets the old banned one back. This tab reproduces the exact, proven injection script instead.

Step 1

⬇️ Backup city & login

Snapshots your current save and login state so you can always return to it. Required — injecting overwrites your current save.

Step 2

🆔 New device ID Do this before injecting

Resets the Android ID and wipes the GSF id first. Skip it and the server simply re-links your fresh city straight back to the old ban.

Sign out of every Google account on the emulator and turn off Play Games cloud backup first — otherwise the banned city restores itself or auto-signs back in.
Step 3

💉 Inject Level-1 city

Stops the game, clears the save folder, then writes a verified fresh pair — byte-checked before anything is reported as success. Root is required; if it cannot verify, it raises instead of pretending.

Step 4

🎮 Open the game

Launch Township so it mints the new city. If a cloud restore offer appears, DECLINE it.

Step 5

✅ Verify fresh city

Pulls the city back and checks that it is genuinely new and at level 1. Green means you are ready to play.

What a local wipe cannot lift: bans tied to a linked Facebook/Google account, emulator hardware fingerprints, or the “YOU'RE USING AN EMULATOR” notice — that one is Playrix policy, not a ban. Those need a fresh login or a brand-new emulator instance (MEmu multi-instance → New, not Clone).
06

🛡️ Safety — What Runs Before Every Push

Five independent gates sit between your edit and your device. Each one refuses only what this tool newly broke — a save that arrived with an oddity keeps it and stays pushable.

🪪 Identity Gate

Refuses a save that declares another player as its owner — a foreign cityId, deviceId, mainPlayer or saveId. This is the one proven instant-ban vector.

📐 Save-Shape Gate

Unbalanced XML, avatar ids outside the real range, a sticker list with the wrong comma shape, an upgrade whose slx doesn't match its level, an integer var holding text, population over its own cap.

📈 Progression Gate

A lifetime counter or a factory level walking backwards without a reason, or a level rising while its experience does not.

⛵ Regatta Gate

Refuses a fabricated task batch — invented ids, uniform scores, dates outside the window, a completed task left on the offer list, records with no tally.

🃏 Card Block Gate

Refuses a CardCollections block that vanishes, appears from nowhere, or breaks an invariant real cities keep.

↩️ Automatic Rollback

If any gate refuses, your save is rolled back to the state it was in before — no half-applied edits, no stuck session. The reason is printed in the console.

✅ The safe operating process

  • Back up first — Export current XML before your first edit.
  • Let the account age — play it past day one before a big copy.
  • Copy in 3 stages — never one giant push onto a newborn.
  • Unlock before you send — own the sticker before using it in co-op.
  • Join quietly — text first, stickers and requests later.
  • One clean device per account — a flagged device re-bans on its own.
  • Read the console — a refusal always says exactly why.
  • Don't stack everything at once — give the game time to load between stages.
  • Verify against the server — fetch your own cityId and compare it to your device after a push.
  • Keep the backup — it reverts anything that misbehaves in game.
🛑 The honest limit — please read this. No tool can guarantee 100% protection against a ban. A green gate means “nothing provably wrong was found in the file” — never “cannot be banned”. Playrix keeps its own server-side record of every account, and behaviour it can see from outside the file still matters. Use this tool responsibly, on accounts you can afford to lose, and never on an account you cannot replace.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

The most common messages, decoded.

MessageWhat it means & what to do
⚠️ EXE closes with “return code -1” Disk full, not corrupt. The portable EXE unpacks ~300 MB into %TEMP% each launch. Free some space and start the tool again.
📵 Device not found Start the emulator, enable ADB, and make sure no other ADB server is holding the port. Reconnect, then press Connect & load again.
🌐 FetchCity failed Usually the app version or the cityId. A “no data” answer simply means Playrix stores no city for that id — the player never synced, or it isn't a city id.
🛑 “Không đẩy file lên máy — …” A push gate refused the edit. The text after the dash names the exact rule. Your save was rolled back; fix the value it names and push again.
😴 “No workshop changed” Every selected row was already at the target level — a genuine no-op, not an error.
🧩 “This file is not a city” The fetched file has no town layout. Pick a different donor, or fetch again.
🎯 Regatta button greyed out The reason is printed directly under it — no active regatta, no template, window closed, already full, or over the daily quota.
🚫 “EXE too old” The tool is older than the server expects — the New Account tab needs a newer bridge. Use a current IGG.VIP.TOOL.zip.

❓ Frequently Asked

🤔 Does editing my save get me banned?
Any modification carries risk — that is the honest truth. What the five gates guarantee is that the file leaving this tool contains nothing provably wrong. They refuse a save that would contradict itself in a field a server can read for free. They cannot see Playrix's own records of your account, and they cannot see how old your account is or what device it sits on. Never treat a green gate as “cannot be banned”.
💾 Where is my backup and how do I restore it?
Press ⬇️ Export current XML any time — it returns the save exactly as it arrived (before any edit in that session). Save that file somewhere safe outside the tool. To go back, write it to your device or start a fresh session and load it.
🧩 Why do some tabs sit behind “⋯ More”?
Eight primary tabs are always visible so labels never wrap or squeeze. The rest — Regatta, Barn, Museum, Zoo, Upgrades and New Account — live in the overflow. Nothing is hidden; it is a layout choice to keep the bar readable on any screen.
🔁 Do I press Save & push after every single change?
No — that is the point of the queue. Make changes across as many tabs as you like, watch the badge count climb, then press 💾 Save & push once. Every queued action lands together and every gate runs over the final result.
⛵ Why is the Regatta Add button greyed out?
Because your save genuinely cannot take the batch — the tab prints the exact reason under the button. Common ones: your city has no active regatta window, there is no completed task to clone, the week is already full, or the batch would exceed the daily quota read from your own save. The badge above shows Templates · Pool · Daily limit so you can see the state before pressing anything.
🏗️ Can I raise a factory past what my city ever had?
No, deliberately. The ceiling is the highest level already in your save (with reference caps of 53 / 31 / 31 when your save has no data yet). A level your city never reached cannot be shown to be valid — writing one would create a contradiction a server can read instantly.
🙋 What happens if a gate refuses my push?
Nothing is written to your device. Your save is rolled back to exactly how it was before that push, the other queued actions are not half-applied, and the System Console prints the precise reason. Fix the value it names and push again — your session stays fully usable.
🌍 Which languages are supported?
Twenty — including English, Vietnamese, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Russian, Turkish, Arabic, Hindi, Thai, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Indonesian, Dutch, Polish, Ukrainian and Malay. Switch from the top bar; all fourteen tabs translate live.
